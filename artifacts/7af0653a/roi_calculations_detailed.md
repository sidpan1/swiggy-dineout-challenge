## Mathematical ROI Optimization Model

### Budget Allocation Formula:
```
Optimal Budget % = (Channel ROI × Scalability Factor) / Σ(All Channel Scores)

Where:
- Scalability Factor = 1 - (Current Spend / Max Effective Spend)
- Max Effective Spend = Point where ROI drops below 5x
```
