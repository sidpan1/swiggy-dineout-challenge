## 1. MENU ENGINEERING TACTICS

### A. Strategic Combo Creation (Impact: +₹120-150 per cover)

**"Spice Garden Signatures" - Curated Meal Experiences**
- **Lunch Express Combo** (₹399): Main + Dal/Curry + Rice/Bread + Dessert
  - Current à la carte: ₹450-480
  - Perceived savings: ₹51-81
  - Margin protection: Bundle lower-cost items

- **Evening Indulgence** (₹649): Starter + Main + Accompaniments + Dessert + Beverage
  - Current à la carte: ₹750-800
  - Perceived savings: ₹101-151
  - Upsell from single main (₹350) to full experience

- **Weekend Family Feast** (₹1,899 for 4): 2 Starters + 4 Mains + Sides + 2 Desserts
  - Per person: ₹475 (below current avg but drives volume)
  - Increases party size and total bill

### B. Beverage Program Optimization (Impact: +₹60-80 per cover)

**Premium Beverage Strategy**
- **Signature Mocktails** (₹149-199)
  - "Koramangala Cooler" - Exclusive to location
  - Cost: ₹30-40, Margin: 75-80%
  
- **Traditional Drinks Bar** (₹89-129)
  - Fresh Lassi variations
  - Masala Chaas specials
  - Sol Kadi, Aam Panna
  
- **Table Water Upsell**
  - Default to premium bottled water (₹49)
  - Offer "Infused Water of the Day" (₹79)

### C. Dessert Attachment Strategy (Impact: +₹50-70 per cover)

**"Sweet Endings" Program**
- **Mini Dessert Platter** (₹199): 3 bite-sized traditional sweets
  - Lower price point increases attachment rate
  - Shareable format for groups
  
- **Dessert + Coffee Combo** (₹249)
  - Bundle pricing saves ₹50
  - Increases average transaction value

## 2. PSYCHOLOGICAL PRICING STRATEGIES

### A. Anchor Pricing
- List one "Chef's Special Thali" at ₹899 (top of menu)
- Makes ₹399-499 items seem reasonable
- 15% will actually order the premium option

### B. Charm Pricing Adjustments
- Change ₹350 → ₹349
- Change ₹400 → ₹399
- Change ₹500 → ₹499
- Psychological impact without real price increase

### C. Menu Layout Optimization
- **Golden Triangle**: Place high-margin items in top-right
- **Box Highlights**: Feature combo meals
- **Photography**: Only for premium/combo items

## 7. MARGIN PROTECTION STRATEGIES

### Smart Bundling Rules
- Never bundle two high-cost proteins
- Always include one high-margin item (beverage/dessert)
- Bread/rice costs negligible - use liberally in combos

### Portion Control for Combos
- Reduce main course portion by 15% in combos
- Customer still feels full due to multiple items
- Saves ₹20-30 per combo in food cost

### Dynamic Pricing
- Use Swiggy Dineout data to adjust combo prices
- Raise prices 5% on high-demand days
- Increase discount during slow periods
